Hydraulic Selection Method

Hydraulic fit is the primary criterion. Operating-speed quality is a tiered gate rather than a weighted score. The calculator selects from the highest available tier first, then compares curve position and frequency reserve.

The final selection score weights hydraulic selection quality at 85% and the estimated energy score at 15%. This prevents a theoretically lower-energy pump from displacing a substantially better hydraulic selection.

Curve position is a field-selection proxy, not Pentair Best Efficiency Point data. Pentair-certified BHP, pump-efficiency contours, and NPSHr are not included.

60 Hz Comparison Baseline

For each configuration, the calculator compares estimated power at the required VFD frequency with the same number and model of pumps running at 60 Hz. This isolates the estimated effect of speed reduction for that configuration.

Example: 2× EQ750 at 48 Hz is compared with 2× EQ750 at 60 Hz—not with a different pump model.

Energy Estimate Method

Estimated shaft power = motor nameplate HP × (required Hz ÷ 60)³. Estimated electrical input then applies the user-entered motor and VFD efficiencies.

These values are useful for relative screening only and are not manufacturer-certified electrical-consumption predictions.
